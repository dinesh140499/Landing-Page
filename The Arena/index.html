01Overview

Pimpri's first sports township

Sport, movement and everyday wellbeing — planned into every hour, for every age.

Mahalaxmi The Arena – PCMC, Pune

Welcome to Mahalaxmi The Arena, a landmark residential development by Mahalaxmi Group in the thriving locale of PCMC, Pune. Spread across a sprawling 16-acre land parcel, this premium township is thoughtfully designed to offer an elevated lifestyle with modern architecture, expansive open spaces, and world-class amenities.

Featuring 11 elegantly crafted towers, Mahalaxmi The Arena offers spacious 2, 3 & 4 BHK apartments that blend comfort, luxury, and convenience. Residents can enjoy a host of 30+ luxurious lifestyle amenities designed for recreation, wellness, fitness, and social gatherings. With excellent connectivity to major business hubs, educational institutions, healthcare facilities, and entertainment zones, the project presents an ideal opportunity for both homebuyers and investors.
